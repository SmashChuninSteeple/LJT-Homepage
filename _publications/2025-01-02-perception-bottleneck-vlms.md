This work studies the perception bottleneck of vision-language models (VLMs) for chart understanding. I am the first author. The authors are Junteng Liu, Weihao Zeng, Xiwen Zhang, Yijun Wang, Zifei Shan, and Junxian He. A GitHub code repository (Vision4Chart) is available.
